# Storyboard (60 s, default timing)

All data is fictional. A "Concept demo • Sample data" label stays visible throughout.

| # | Scene | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Problem | 0–6 s | Headline "Great products. Too many messages." Three message cards land (Messenger, WhatsApp, Instagram) with faint ghost cards for a busy feel. |
| 2 | Unified inbox | 6–14 s | "Every conversation. One workspace." The cards fly into an inbox list; a dashboard appears with list, conversation and customer panel. Maya is selected, the view zooms to her conversation and the customer panel is outlined. |
| 3 | Conversation | 14–29 s | Large phone. Maya asks about the navy hoodie in L; typing dots; assistant confirms size L at $49 (an inventory note shows the data source); product card; "I'll take one."; "Where should we send it?"; an order-draft note; the chat collapses into "Delivery details received". |
| 4 | Order approval | 29–41 s | The phone shrinks away. "From conversation to order. You approve before it moves forward." Order card #1042: Hoodie, L, qty 1, $49 + $5 = $54, **Pending approval**. Cursor clicks **Approve order** → **Approved**. No payment or shipping is shown. |
| 5 | Human takeover | 41–50 s | "Automation, with you in control." Hannah asks for a return-policy exception. AI shows "preparing a reply"; **Needs your attention** appears. Cursor clicks **Take over** → **Human handling**, "Automated replies paused", auto-replies Paused. |
| 6 | Overview | 50–56 s | "A clear view of every conversation." Tiles count to the post-demo state: 6 open, 5 AI, 1 human, 1 order approved; channel breakdown. The changed tiles are outlined. |
| 7 | Closing | 56–60 s | InboxPilot wordmark, "More time for your business.", "AI-assisted conversations. Human-controlled orders.", dashboard + phone, footer "Product concept • Prototype demonstration". Calm hold. |

Transitions: scenes cross-fade through the background (0.4 s). Scene 1→2 (message cards) and 3→4 (phone) hand objects over without a fade, so the total stays exactly 60 s.
